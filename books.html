Henry 5, Act1

I read the synopsis for each scene before reading it. The story is straightforward, but the lanaguage is so intensive. Reading one Act at a time is a good habit I feel. The Bishops of Ely and Canterbury have good reason for King Henry to wave war on France. The bill requiring them to pay more taxes will be buried under the intensity of war. King Henry is thinking of taking France, as he has a slight claim to the throne. The heir of France sends tennis balls as a gift to Henry as satire, but Henry will not stand for the mockery and vows to go to war with France, while leaving 3/4 of his forces home to defend against possible Scot attacks.

There is a podcast called "THe Play's the Thing" that goes over all Shakespeare's works. Read an act before, and listen to the podcast at work.
